## How to reach us

The best way to contact NanakShahi is by email. Messages are read by the NanakShahi Editorial Team. We aim to reply to every message, but please allow a few days for a response, especially around major Gurpurabs.

## Reporting a correction

Accuracy matters to us. If you believe an article contains an error, please include:

- the title or link of the article;
- the passage you believe is incorrect;
- what you believe is correct, with a source if possible.

We review every correction request. Where we agree that a change is needed, we update the article and its "Updated" date. See our [editorial standards](/about#editorial-standards) for more about how we handle corrections.

## Suggesting a topic

Is there a Gurpurab, festival, historical event or term you would like explained? Let us know. Reader questions shape much of what we publish.

## What we cannot help with

- **The daily Hukamnama.** We do not publish the day's Hukamnama. Please see the official sources listed on our [Hukamnama Today](/hukamnama-today) page.
- **Religious rulings.** We cannot advise on questions of Sikh religious practice. Please consult your gurdwara or the relevant Sikh institutions.
- **Gurdwara bookings and services.** NanakShahi is independent and cannot arrange Akhand Paths, accommodation or services at any gurdwara. Please contact the gurdwara directly.

## Privacy

When you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply and to act on your request. We do not add you to mailing lists. See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy) for details.
