## Independent publication

NanakShahi is an independent website. It is **not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated on behalf of** the Shiromani Gurdwara Parbandhak Committee (SGPC), Sri Akal Takht Sahib, any Takht, any gurdwara or gurdwara management committee, or any government body. Names of institutions and places are used only to describe them.

## Educational information, not religious guidance

Our articles explain Sikh history, observances and traditions for a general audience. They are not religious rulings and are not a substitute for the Sikh Rehat Maryada, the guidance of Sikh institutions or the advice of your local gurdwara. Where Sikh traditions or historians differ, we try to present the different views fairly. Readers should consult authoritative sources on matters of religious practice.

## Gurbani and the Hukamnama

NanakShahi does not publish, retype or paraphrase the daily Hukamnama from Sri Harmandir Sahib or any other gurdwara. Where we discuss the Hukamnama, we explain the tradition and direct readers to official sources. If we ever display Hukamnama text in future, it will come directly from a verified source and will be clearly attributed.

## Dates

Dates of Gurpurabs and Sikh festivals vary depending on the calendar used, and many change every year. Dates mentioned on NanakShahi are provided for general information. Always confirm dates with the SGPC calendar or your local gurdwara before making plans.

## Visiting information

Information about visiting gurdwaras, including Sri Harmandir Sahib, is general guidance. Timings, entry arrangements and rules can change at short notice. Check with official sources before you travel.

## Illustrations

Images on NanakShahi are stylised illustrations created for this website, some with the help of AI image tools. They are **not photographs** and should not be taken as accurate depictions of real people, events or architecture.

Links to other websites are provided for convenience and information. We do not control and are not responsible for the content of external websites.
